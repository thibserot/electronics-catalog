# Aluminium Electrolytic Capacitor Kit (15 values) — PA008

300-piece, 15-value general-purpose (low-frequency) aluminium electrolytic kit (box code **B11**).
For bulk decoupling, supply smoothing and audio coupling. Polarised — mind orientation.
For low-ESR/high-frequency electrolytics see **PA003**.

- **Voltage range:** 16 V – 50 V
- **Frequency:** Low frequency (general purpose)
- **Material:** Aluminium
- **Quantity:** 20 pcs per value (300 total)

## Notes
- **Polarity:** the stripe marks the **negative** lead; the longer lead is **+**. Reverse voltage will damage or pop the cap.
- **Voltage margin:** pick a cap rated well above your rail (e.g. don't put a 16 V part on a 12 V rail with transients — prefer ≥25 V there).
- Electrolytics dry out with age/heat; check ESR if reusing very old stock.
