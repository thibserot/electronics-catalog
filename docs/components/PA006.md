# Metal Film Resistor Kit (50 values) — PA006

50-value **1/4 W (0.25 W) 1%** metal film resistor assortment kit.
Box barcode **81028625**, made in China. Metal-film parts give a tighter tolerance
and lower temperature coefficient than the loose carbon-film box (**PA005**).
